The Bonzy Almanac

Every Project Zomboid recipe marked NeedToBeLearn in the game's generated scripts (export of 1 Nov 2026), and every way to unlock it: auto-learn skill levels, research, literature, and professions/traits.

Research: an item's Researchablerecipes list is explicit. "Research own output" assumes the engine lets you research a recipe from the item it produces unless tagged CannotBeResearched (you confirmed this for the snare). Only two recipes set ResearchSkillLevel; for the rest the level gate is presumably the craft requirement. With a skill + level set, green means you already meet that skill's number.
